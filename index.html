Mooring System - Safety Analysis

The "Tight Rope" Rule

A slack mooring line allows the massive balloon to accelerate. When the inertia hits the end of the line, it acts as a loaded spring. This visualizes the exponential elastic rebound forces inflicted on your hardware.

2. Model Equations Breakdown

True Inertial Mass (\( m_{\text{tot}} \)) : Includes hardware mass and dynamic hydrodynamic added mass (0.6 × Volume).

$$ m_{\text{tot}} = m_{\text{system}} + m_{\text{added\_air}} $$

Kinetic Impact Force (\( F_{\text{impact}} \)) : Converts kinetic energy into elastic potential energy.

$$ F_{\text{impact}} = v \sqrt{m_{\text{tot}} \cdot k} $$

Vectorial Peak Tension (\( T_{\text{max}} \)) : The absolute maximum force on the anchor.

$$ T_{\text{max}} = \sqrt{F_{\text{impact}}^2 + \left( F_z + m_{\text{tot}} \frac{v^2}{L} - m_{\text{basket}} \cdot g \right)^2} $$
