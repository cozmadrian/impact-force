2. Model Equations Breakdown

True Inertial Mass (\( m_{\text{tot}} \)) : Includes hardware mass and hydrodynamic added mass.

$$ m_{\text{tot}} = m_{\text{system}} + m_{\text{added\_air}} $$

Kinetic Impact Force (\( F_{\text{impact}} \)) : Converts kinetic energy into elastic potential energy.

$$ F_{\text{impact}} = v \sqrt{m_{\text{tot}} \cdot k} $$

Centrifugal Force (\( F_c \)) : Pendulum motion of the envelope around the basket.

$$ F_c = m_{\text{tot}} \frac{v^2}{L} $$

Vectorial Peak Tension (\( T_{\text{max}} \)) : The absolute maximum force on the anchor.

$$ T_{\text{max}} = \sqrt{F_{\text{impact}}^2 + \left( F_z + F_c - m_{\text{basket}} \cdot g \right)^2} $$
